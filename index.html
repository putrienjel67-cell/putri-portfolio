How I Work

A clear path from request to handoff.

  1. Understand

    Review the request, required fields, source files, and expected output.

  2. Organize

    Structure working data, naming, references, and the review process.

  3. Check

    Validate completeness, consistency, duplicates, totals, and exceptions.

  4. Deliver

    Prepare the final output with clear notes and items that still need review.
